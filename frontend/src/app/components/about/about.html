About SmartNews

SmartNews keeps you on top of tech news without the manual digging. It automatically pulls in the latest articles from TechCrunch, The Verge, and Ars Technica every hour, then reads each one for you — pulling out the people, companies, places, dates and figures it mentions, predicting its topic, and scoring its sentiment — so you can search, filter, and find what matters faster than skimming three separate sites.

What it does for you

  • Always up to date — new articles from all three sources are fetched automatically, hourly.
  • Search beyond the headline — full-text search covers article titles and body text, not just titles.
  • Filter by who, what, where, and tone — narrow results by person, organization, location, date, or quantity mentioned in an article, by predicted topic, and by whether its overall sentiment is positive, neutral, or negative.
  • Know the topic and tone at a glance — every article is automatically classified (business, tech, sport, entertainment, or politics) and scored for sentiment, with a visual breakdown on its page. Click a topic anywhere it appears to jump straight to every other article on that topic.
  • Know the time commitment — each article shows an estimated reading time and word count on its page before you dive in.
  • Discover related coverage — every article links to up to 5 others that share its entities, topic, or tone.
  • Take your search with you — export the articles matching your current filters to a CSV file at any time.
  • Save articles for later — bookmark anything and come back to it anytime from the Saved page.
  • See the big picture — the Stats page charts article volume by topic, source, and sentiment over the last 30 days, plus the most frequently mentioned people, organizations, and locations.
  • See what's fresh — the Pipeline page shows the history of every scrape run (scheduled or manual) and lets you kick off a new one on demand.

How to use it

  1. Browse — the Articles page lists the latest stories. Use the source tabs (All / Techcrunch / Theverge / Arstechnica) to narrow the feed to one outlet, and the newest/oldest toggle to flip sort order.
  2. Search — use the search bar in the top bar to search across article titles and text.
  3. Filter — open Advanced filters (the sliders icon) on the Articles page to search by title, body text, sentiment, predicted topic, or by entity: persons, organizations, locations, dates & times, or quantities & percentages. Clicking any entity, topic, or sentiment segment on an article's page jumps straight to the filtered list.
  4. Export — click the download icon above the article list to download every article matching your current filters, not just the current page.
  5. Read — click any article to see its full details: author, source, reading time, predicted topic, a sentiment breakdown chart, tags, extracted entities, related articles, and the full article text, plus a link back to the original.
  6. Save for later — click the bookmark icon on a story or an article's page to bookmark it. Find everything you've saved anytime on the Saved page.
  7. Explore trends — visit the Stats page for a bird's-eye view: a 30-day sentiment trend, article counts by topic and source, the overall sentiment split, and the top mentioned people, organizations, and locations across every article.
  8. Check on scraping — visit the Pipeline page to see recent scrape runs and their status, or trigger a new run yourself with Run now.
