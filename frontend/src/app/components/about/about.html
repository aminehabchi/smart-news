About NLP Scraper

NLP Scraper keeps you on top of tech news without the manual digging. It automatically pulls in the latest articles from TechCrunch, The Verge, and Ars Technica every hour, then reads each one for you — pulling out the people, companies, places, dates and figures it mentions, and predicting its topic — so you can search, filter, and find what matters faster than skimming three separate sites.

What it does for you

  • Always up to date — new articles from all three sources are fetched automatically, hourly.
  • Search beyond the headline — full-text search covers article titles and body text, not just titles.
  • Filter by who, what, and where — narrow results by person, organization, location, date, or quantity mentioned in an article, not just keywords.
  • Know the topic at a glance — every article is automatically classified (business, tech, sport, entertainment, or politics) and tagged with the entities it mentions.
  • Know the time commitment — each article shows an estimated reading time before you click in.
  • Save articles for later — bookmark anything and come back to it anytime from the Saved page.

How to use it

  1. Browse — the Articles page lists the latest stories. Use the source chips (All / Techcrunch / Theverge / Arstechnica) to narrow the feed to one outlet.
  2. Search — use the search bar in the top bar to search across article titles and text.
  3. Filter — open Advanced filters on the Articles page to search by title, body text, or by entity: persons, organizations, locations, dates & times, or quantities & percentages.
  4. Read — click any article to see its full details: author, source, reading time, predicted topic, tags, extracted entities, and the full article text, plus a link back to the original.
  5. Save for later — click + Save for later on an article's page to bookmark it. Find everything you've saved anytime on the Saved page.
